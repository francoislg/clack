# Cron Jobs: Universal Redacted Visibility + Editable-By-Anyone Flag

## Why

Two live incidents followed the same shape: a user asked Clack to turn off "this automation" in a channel where two similar scheduled jobs existed, owned by two different people. Because `list_scheduled_messages` hides other users' jobs from non-admins, Clack resolved the request against an incomplete list — the only job it could see was the requester's own — and cancelled the wrong one. Twice.

The permission layer conflates two different concerns: *content privacy* (a job's prompt can contain private instructions) and *existence visibility* (knowing a job exists, whose it is, and when it fires). Hiding existence makes cross-user disambiguation structurally impossible and hides shared team automations from the people relying on them.

## What Changes

1. **Universal redacted visibility.** `list_scheduled_messages` lists ALL channel-targeted jobs for every caller. Rows the caller cannot fully view (not admin, not owner, job not `editableByAnyone`) are redacted to identity/schedule metadata — `id`, `name`, `channel`, human schedule + cron expression, `enabled`, `oneShot`, owner (`createdBy` / `systemActor`), `plugin`, `editableByAnyone`, `lastRunAt`, `lastRunStatus` — and flagged `redacted: true`. Content fields (`prompt`, `skipConditions`, `requiredTools`, `attachedTopics`, `submitResponseMode`, `attentionLevel`, run details) are omitted on redacted rows. **DM privacy carve-out:** jobs targeting a DM (channel starts with `D`) and channelless user-created jobs stay visible only to their owner and admins — personal automations don't leak existence. The admin-only `includeOtherUsers` arg is removed — the scope is now always-all-visible; the `channel` / `plugin` filters are unchanged. `get_scheduled_message` on a non-viewable job returns the redacted summary plus guidance (name the owner; suggest asking them or an admin) instead of an error.

2. **`editableByAnyone` flag** — the `userSkills` precedent applied to cron jobs. Optional boolean on `CronJob`, settable at create and toggleable only by the job's owner or an admin (via `update_scheduled_message` or a Home Tab toggle). When `true`, ANY user gets full visibility of the job and can `update` (content AND enable/disable) and `run-now` it. **Delete stays owner/admin-only** — `cancel_scheduled_message` never opens up via the flag; the recoverable off-switch for shared jobs is disabling. Plugin-managed jobs can never carry the flag. The Home Tab shows a shared badge on flagged rows plus the owner/admin toggle button.

3. **Home Tab: "Shared" terminology + grouped sections, for Skills AND Schedules.** The user-facing label for `editableByAnyone` becomes **"Shared"** everywhere (the skills badge today reads "(editable by everyone)"). Both the Skills section and the Scheduled Messages section regroup into up to three viewer-relative subsections, each with its own header and rendered only when non-empty: **Shared** (flagged items, at the top), **Yours** (items you own, not shared), and **Non-Accessible** (other users' non-shared items — labeled "Other users'" for admins, who can act on them). For schedules, the Non-Accessible rows are the redacted projection (name, channel, schedule, owner — no Edit button for non-admins) and others' DM-targeted/channelless jobs stay hidden entirely; the Home Tab thereby mirrors the tool-layer visibility exactly. The admin-only plugin-managed schedules subsection is unchanged.

4. **Disable becomes a first-class tool path, and "turn off" steers to it.** `update_scheduled_message` gains an `enabled` field (today the tools offer no way to disable — "turn this off" could only ever be a delete). `cancel_scheduled_message`'s result echoes the cancelled job's `name`, `channel`, schedule, and owner, and the tool descriptions instruct Claude to (a) confirm by job NAME and owner — never "this automation" — whenever more than one job targets the channel in scope, and (b) prefer disable over delete when the user says "turn off"/"stop" without explicit delete wording. This is the direct fix for the incident shape even after visibility lands.

## Impact

- **Specs:** `cron-messages` (data model + tool requirements + schedules Home Tab grouping), `user-created-skills` (badge rename + skills Home Tab grouping).
- **Code:** `src/cronJobs.ts` (type + zod + serialization), new shared access-predicate module under `src/tools/`, `src/tools/query/listScheduledMessages.ts`, `getScheduledMessage.ts`, `getScheduledMessageRuns.ts`, `src/tools/actions/createScheduledMessage.ts`, `updateScheduledMessage.ts`, `cancelScheduledMessage.ts`, `runScheduledMessageNow.ts`, `src/slack/homeTab.ts` (scheduled-messages section), `src/slack/userSkillsHomeTab.ts`, i18n strings (en + fr), scheduling instruction topic, tests throughout.
- **No migration:** `editableByAnyone` is absent-means-false on a graceful zod reader.
